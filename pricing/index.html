Pricing

The Labmill client is free. A Labmill account costs 6 USD a month, or 35 USD a month for a fully private account.

Payment is not open yet. Card payment through Stripe is coming soon.

Client

Free

The Labmill program on your computer. It uses your own cluster login and your own Anthropic API key. It works without a Labmill account.

A Labmill account includes

  • Sign-in with Google or GitHub.
  • Your jobs: the client sends job records to your account, and the app shows them. A job record holds only scheduler facts, not your files, code or data.
  • Teams and RSE units: research software engineers (RSEs) of your institution can help you, and you can send them help requests.
  • Program tokens for the client, which you make and revoke in Settings.

Without an account, you can browse more than 200 HPC systems and search the public facts. Each fact has its source, the date it was read and a confidence.

Institutions

A plan for a university, institute or research computing center, by agreement. RSEs and administrators of your institution need a verified institutional email address and a DNS check. They see a user's jobs only when the institution allows it for all users and the user shares the job.

Talk to us about your institution: help@labmill.org

Questions

Does the client cost money?
No. The Labmill client is free and open source under the MIT license.
Why does the fully private account cost more?
Redacted job facts from Labmill accounts make Labmill better for everyone. A fully private account does not give these facts, so it pays more of the cost.
Can I change later?
Yes. Change the contribution setting in Settings.
Is tax included?
No. The prices are in USD. We add sales tax or VAT where the law requires it.
Can I cancel?
Yes. When payment is open, you can cancel at any time in Settings. You must cancel before you delete your account.

Other questions: write to help@labmill.org.
