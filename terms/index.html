The service

Labmill (app.labmill.org) shows your HPC jobs, gives help from a knowledge base of cluster facts, and lets research software engineers (RSEs) support the users of their institution. By signing in you accept these terms and the privacy policy.

Your account

  • Sign in with Google or GitHub. Keep your account safe. You are responsible for what happens under it.
  • RSE and administrator rights need a verified institutional email and a DNS check. We can remove these rights when the check fails.
  • You can delete your account in Settings (see the privacy policy for what happens).

What you must not do

  • Upload export-controlled, classified, patient or other restricted data, or any data you may not share. Redaction of logs is not perfect and can miss names.
  • Break the rules of your cluster or institution, attack the service, or try to see data of others.
  • Use another person's identity or institutional email.

Your data and contribution

You keep your rights in your data. You give us the right to store and process it to run the service. If your contribution setting is on, redacted job data can feed anonymous statistics and general lessons for all users. What went into those, without your identity, stays after you leave. We do not train models on your data. A subscription costs 6 USD a month, or 35 USD a month for a fully private account (contribution off), plus tax where it applies.

Visibility to RSEs

RSEs and administrators of your tenant can see your shared jobs when the tenant allows it for all users. You can keep a job private. See the privacy policy.

Payment

Paid plans are billed by our payment provider. Cancel at any time in Settings. You must cancel before you delete your account.

No guarantee

Answers and suggestions from Labmill can be wrong. Check them before you run jobs or change a cluster. The service is given as is, with no promise of uptime. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect loss, and our total liability is the amount you paid in the last 12 months.

Changes and end

We can change these terms; we will tell you in the app before a material change. We can suspend an account that breaks these terms. You can stop at any time.
