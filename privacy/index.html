Labmill (app.labmill.org) helps researchers use HPC clusters. This page says what data we keep, why, and how you can remove it.

Sign-in data

You sign in with Google or GitHub. From Google we ask for the scopes openid, email and profile. We keep your provider account ID, your email address, whether the provider says the email is verified, and your name. We do not read your contacts, files, calendar or mail. We use the email and name to run your account and to send you messages about it.

The session cookie is needed for sign-in. We set no advertising or tracking cookies.

On the systems page, the server uses your IP address to estimate an approximate city, to sort the systems by distance. It uses the DB-IP database on our own server. We do not store the IP address.

Institutional email

You can sign in with any account. To act as a research software engineer (RSE) or administrator for an institution, you must prove that you control an email address at that institution's domain, and then that the institution's DNS holds a record we ask for. Free-mail domains do not count. We keep the address and the result of the check.

Job data and redaction

If you use the Labmill client, it sends job records: cluster, state, exit code, failure category, time, resources requested and used, and software names. For failed jobs it can send a few redacted log lines. Redaction runs on your computer first, and again on our server. It removes paths, user and host names, IP addresses, emails, tokens, keys and long numbers. The client never sends your files, code or data.

Redaction can miss. An error line can still name a gene, a compound or a project. Do not use Labmill for export-controlled or other restricted data.

Who can see your jobs

  • You see all your own jobs.
  • RSEs and administrators of your tenant (your institution's group) see a job only when the tenant allows it for all users and the job is shared. You can keep a job private. In tenants that use opt-in, they see nothing until you share a job.
  • Help requests and messages you send to RSEs are visible to them.
  • If your contribution setting is on, redacted jobs can feed anonymous statistics and general lessons. These carry no name or email. Turning it off changes your price (see the terms). We do not train models on your data.

Crawler data

Separate from user data, our crawler (user agent LabmillBot, see labmill.org/bot/) reads public HPC documentation pages. It follows robots.txt, goes slowly, and keeps an exclusion list. We store facts and short quotes with links to the source. It is about institutions and systems, not about people. A site can ask us to exclude it at the contact address below.

Services that process data for us

  • Hetzner (Falkenstein, Germany): runs our server and database.
  • Amazon Web Services (US West, Oregon): encrypted backups, outgoing and incoming email (SES) and DNS.
  • Fireworks AI and Anthropic: language models that read crawled public pages to extract and check facts. We do not send your account data to them.
  • Brave Search: gets search queries to find public HPC pages. No user data.
  • Stripe: card payments, when billing is on. We never see your card number.
  • Google, GitHub: sign-in, under your choice.

Your data is processed in Germany and the United States, also if you live elsewhere.

How long we keep data

  • Job records: 3 years. Redacted log lines: 1 year.
  • Account and consent records: while the account exists.
  • Backups: 35 days.

Deleting your account

In Settings you can delete your account. Cancel any subscription first. At once, you are signed out for good, your name and email are blanked, tokens and consents end, and your learning jobs are turned off. After 30 days we remove your jobs, log lines, usage, subscription records and verified emails. What already went into anonymous statistics or confirmed facts stays, without you.

You can ask for a copy of your data or for a correction at the contact address.
